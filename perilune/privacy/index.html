Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Perilune is a game about a small piece of the Moon finding its way home. It runs entirely on your device, and it was made by an independent developer ("we", "us") to ask nothing of you but your attention.

Data we collect

None. Perilune does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or software development kits.

Your progress

The nights you have reached, the gleams you have gathered and your settings are saved in the game's storage on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

If you use iCloud, your progress (not your settings) is also kept in your own iCloud account, so your iPhone, iPad and Apple TV can continue the same journey. Apple stores it under its own privacy policy; we have no server and cannot see it.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you are signed in on your device, the achievements you earn are recorded by Apple's Game Center service under Apple's privacy policy. Perilune does not receive, store or share your Game Center information, and the game plays the same without it.

Children

Perilune collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Deleting the game removes its data from your device; the progress copy in iCloud belongs to your Apple Account, where Apple handles it under its own policy. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK) you may have further rights; write to us and we will help.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that uses data, this page will be updated before that version is released and will say exactly what is used and why.
