Portolino Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Portolino ("the game") is a puzzle game for iOS and Android made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. We do not have user accounts, and we do not run our own servers or analytics.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal data on our own servers. Your game progress, settings and purchases status are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID / IDFA, where permitted), approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This data is processed by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Consent: Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings.
  • iOS tracking: On iOS, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Younger players: The game asks for your birth year. Players under 13 only receive non-personalized ads, and no advertising identifier is used for them.
  • Removing ads: Buying "Remove Ads" turns off interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads stay available only when you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, so the game can unlock it.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Players who say they are under 13 only receive non-personalized ads.

Your rights

Because we store no personal data, deleting the game removes all of your game data from your device. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings and manage consent in the game's Settings. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have further rights; contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
